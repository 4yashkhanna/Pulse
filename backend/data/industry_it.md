# Industry Pack — IT & Technology

Calibration notes for technology and software organisations. The coach uses these to
frame frameworks in the language and constraints of an IT team.

## IT — Validate the Problem Before Building
<!-- meta: framework=IT Problem Validation; pillar=behavior; phase=define; industry=it -->
Technology teams have high AI literacy and ship fast — which means the failure mode is
building the wrong thing efficiently. The leverage point is pushing them from "build quickly"
toward "validate the right problem first." Frame empathy and problem-definition work as a way
to reduce expensive post-launch rework, not as a delay. Costly pivots after launch are the
risk this sector most needs to hear about.

## IT — Service Blueprinting for Platforms
<!-- meta: framework=IT Service Blueprint; pillar=resources; phase=prototype; industry=it -->
In IT, journeys span APIs, services, and teams. A service blueprint is especially valuable
because the backstage (systems, integrations, on-call ownership) is where platform
experiences break. When an IT team is scoping a multi-service feature, blueprinting at the
design stage surfaces ownership gaps and failure modes before they become production
incidents.

## IT — Prototype with Real Constraints
<!-- meta: framework=IT Prototyping; pillar=process; phase=prototype; industry=it -->
Encourage clickable or technical spikes that test the riskiest assumption (a latency budget,
an integration, a workflow) rather than polished UI. The question is usually "will this
technically and experientially hold up," and the cheapest spike that answers it wins.
