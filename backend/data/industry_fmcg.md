# Industry Pack — FMCG & Consumer

Calibration notes for fast-moving consumer goods and consumer brands.

## FMCG — Evidence Over Aesthetic Instinct
<!-- meta: framework=FMCG Evidence; pillar=success; phase=test; industry=fmcg -->
FMCG teams usually have strong aesthetic design culture already. The leverage point is
pushing them from "this looks right" toward evidence grounded in real shopper behaviour —
shelf tests, pack research, and purchase data. Frame evidence validation as protecting a
creative bet, not overruling it: the instinct stays, but it gets a landing zone before the
print run.

## FMCG — Shopper Empathy in Context
<!-- meta: framework=FMCG Shopper Empathy; pillar=values; phase=empathy; industry=fmcg -->
Consumer decisions happen in seconds, in-aisle or in-app, under distraction. Empathy work
for FMCG means observing real shopping behaviour in context, not focus-group opinions in a
room. The gap between stated preference and actual purchase is large in this sector, so
behavioural observation beats self-report.

## FMCG — Test Concepts Cheaply and Early
<!-- meta: framework=FMCG Concept Testing; pillar=process; phase=test; industry=fmcg -->
Pack, naming, and concept directions can be tested with low-fidelity stimuli before any
production commitment. Encourage multiple distinct concepts tested against real shoppers
early — narrow exploration is the biggest predictor of a launch that misses the segment it
was meant for.
